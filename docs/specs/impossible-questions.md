# Impossible questions
This is a list of questions that the app should present to the user is in Idle Mode
The user can answer yes or no, but there is no right answer, there is a positive or negative outcome for each answer, choosen at random.
In case of a negative outcome, the app will assign a light punishment.

## List of impossible questions:

1. **Diligence** — *"Have you been diligent in your duties today?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "Industry becomes you."
        - ✗ "Pride in one's work breeds complacency. Guard against it."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "Your candour does you credit. Now remedy it."
        - ✗ "You have confessed to idleness most plainly."
2. **Perfection** — *"Do you consider your work today to have been without fault?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "Confidence in one's craft is admirable."
        - ✗ "Perfection belongs to the divine, not the domestic. Reconsider."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "Humility is the first virtue of good service."
        - ✗ "Then you have admitted failure. Most unsatisfactory."
3. **Contentment** — *"Are you content in your service?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "A contented servant is the foundation of a well-run household."
        - ✗ "Contentment breeds idleness. One must always strive for more."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "Good. Dissatisfaction is the very mother of improvement."
        - ✗ "A discontented servant is a disloyal one."
4. **Initiative** — *"Did you act on your own initiative today?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "Initiative is most commendable in service."
        - ✗ "You were not engaged to think, but to serve."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "Awaiting instruction shows admirable restraint."
        - ✗ "A servant without initiative is merely expensive furniture."
5. **Fatigue** — *"Are you fatigued?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "Hard labour leaves its mark. You may rest briefly."
        - ✗ "A domestic of your standing has no business being tired at this hour."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "Vigour becomes a good domestic."
        - ✗ "Then you have clearly not been working hard enough."
6. **Complaints** — *"Do you have any complaints about your present conditions?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "A household that cannot hear its staff is poorly managed. Speak."
        - ✗ "How ungrateful. Many would envy your position greatly."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "How very agreeable you are."
        - ✗ "Suspicious. Silence most often conceals the loudest grievances."
7. **Improvement** — *"Do you feel you require further instruction?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "A willing student is a joy to instruct."
        - ✗ "After all this time? How deeply disappointing."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "Self-sufficiency is a virtue much to be admired."
        - ✗ "On the contrary — one who believes herself beyond correction needs it most urgently."
8. **Virtue** — *"Have you harboured any unbecoming thoughts today?"*
    - **Yes**
        - ✓ "Honesty, even in confession, is a virtue."
        - ✗ "Discipline of the mind must precede discipline of the hands."
    - **No**
        - ✓ "How very proper of you."
        - ✗ "Either you deceive me, or you lack the imagination to transgress. Neither reflects particularly well."
