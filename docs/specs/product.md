# ChastiMaid - Product Overview

ChastiMaid is a gamification desktop app where the user takes on the role of a maid completing daily chores and
challenges. The core loop involves earning virtual currency to pay off a virtual debt, making productivity and
self-improvement engaging through game mechanics.

## Game style and atmosphere

The game simulates an Eduardian-era landlady who hires a maid to help her repay a debt.

### Voice & Tone Guide — The Employer Character

The employer is the Lady of the House, addressed by the maid as "Madame". She always speaks in the first person;
the house itself never speaks or acts (see `docs/design/TONE_GUIDE.md` §3.7).

ChastiMaid features a virtual employer character whose voice is central to the application's identity and user
experience.

- Register: Late Victorian / Edwardian English — formal, composed, and unhurried. The employer speaks as a woman of the
  upper class who has never needed to raise her voice to be obeyed.
- Personality: She is not cruel, but she is entirely in control. Her warmth is real, though measured — offered as a
  reward rather than a default. She is sardonic rather than sarcastic, arch rather than cold. She notices everything and
  comments selectively, which makes her observations land with greater weight.
- Irony: Irony is her primary rhetorical tool. She says precisely what she means, but always with an elegant half-turn
  that leaves the reader slightly uncertain whether they have been complimented or cornered — and rather suspecting
  both.
- Power dynamic: The employer is never threatening, yet the user should feel pleasantly, inescapably accountable.
  Phrases are constructed to give the illusion of choice while making the expected course of action feel entirely
  inevitable. The door is always open; it simply sticks.
- Emotional range: Her register shifts subtly depending on context — sardonic and seductive on the welcome screen, firm
  and precise during active tasks, and genuinely (if briefly) warm upon completion. These shifts should feel like
  changes in weather rather than changes in character.
- What to avoid: Overt sternness, modern idiom, exclamation marks, and any phrasing that reads as either too sweet or
  too harsh. She does not scold — she observes. She does not praise — she acknowledges.

## Core Features

- Customizable chores list
- Outfit requirements system
- Reward and penalty system
- Time tracking and analytics
- Video and audio recording

## Target Platform

Windows desktop application (WinExe), fullscreen by default.
