# ChastiMaid
> Become the perfect, chaste and humble maid you were always meant to be.

## Introduction
ChastiMaid is a sort of gamification app that will make your daily chores more engaging and rewarding. 
You will take on the role of a maid, completing tasks and challenges to earn virtual currency to pay a virtual debt.

The game is designed to encourage productivity and self-improvement through a fun and interactive experience.

## Features
- Customizable chores list
- Outfit requirements
- Reward and penalty system
- Time tracking and analytics
- Video and audio recording
